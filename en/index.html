Everything a notebook has, and more

The infinite canvas of Excalidraw with the organization and pens of Samsung Notes, in one app.

Infinite canvas

Shapes, arrows that stick to shapes, text, sticky notes, frames, images and a laser pointer. Unlimited zoom and pinch.

Real pens

Pressure and tilt, five pens (fountain, pencil, highlighter...) and an eraser on the back of your stylus. Hold still at the end of a stroke and it snaps into a perfect shape.

Stay organized

Colored folders, tags, favorites and trash. Accent-insensitive search. Ready-made templates for to-dos, meetings and mind maps.

Paper and PDF

Eleven sheet types: ruled, grid, dotted, Cornell, music staff, chalkboard and more. Import a PDF and write on every page.

Touch and gestures

Palm rejection while you write. Two-finger tap to undo, three-finger tap to redo. Built for tablets and touchscreens.

Your notes, yours only

No mandatory cloud, no account: everything stays on your computer. Lock notes with a password and export to PDF, PNG or SVG.

Take a look

A light paper theme, a chalkboard in dark mode, and a home screen that feels like a shelf of notebooks.

Get it for your system

Free for Windows, Mac and Linux. Pick the installer or the portable version, which runs straight from its folder (even from a USB stick) without installing anything.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

Installer (.exe) Portable (.zip)

Portable: extract the folder and open libreNotes.exe. If Windows says the app isn't recognized, click More info › Run anyway.

macOS

macOS 13 or newer

Apple Silicon (M1–M4) Intel Mac

No install: Apple Silicon .zip · Intel .zip

Open the .dmg and drag the app to Applications. The first time, allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

Linux

64-bit, recent distros

AppImage (portable) Install (.tar.gz)

AppImage: make it executable and open it. To install it in your launcher, without admin rights:

tar xzf libreNotes-*.tar.gz
./libreNotes/install.sh

Linux: GTK 4.20+ and libadwaita 1.8+ (Arch, CachyOS, SteamOS, Fedora 43+, Ubuntu 25.10+ and similar). The Windows and Mac warnings appear only because the app has no paid code signature. All versions and release notes.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask most about libreNotes.

Is libreNotes free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no paid tier and no account. If you like it, you can support the project with a donation.

Does it run on Windows or Mac?

Yes. There is an installer and a portable version for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), and builds for macOS 13 or newer on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. On Linux there is an AppImage and an installable package.

Does it work with a stylus and touchscreen?

Yes. It supports pen pressure and tilt, the eraser tip and the side buttons. It has palm rejection and touch gestures: two-finger tap to undo, three-finger tap to redo.

Can I annotate PDFs?

Yes. When you import a PDF, each page becomes a locked sheet you can write on. You can then export to PDF, PNG or SVG.

Does libreNotes open Excalidraw files?

Yes. It imports .excalidraw files and .excalidrawlib libraries. Notes are saved in its own .lnote format.

Where are my notes stored?

On your computer: in ~/.local/share/librenotes on Linux, %APPDATA%\librenotes on Windows and ~/Library/Application Support/librenotes on Mac. With the portable version, a librenotes-data folder next to the app keeps your notes with it. Nothing goes to the cloud unless you ask, and notes can be password-protected.

Which Linux distributions are supported?

Any distribution with GTK 4.20 or newer and libadwaita 1.8 or newer, such as Arch, CachyOS, SteamOS, Fedora 43+ and Ubuntu 25.10+.
